Introduction

Status 200 respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you visit our website, place an order, or contact us, and how we use it.

1. Data Collection

We only collect the information we need to run our store:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your shipping address and phone number
  • Browsing data, such as the pages you visit on our site

2. Personal Data Usage

We use your information only to serve you:

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To respond to your questions and inquiries

3. Privacy Practices

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is shared only with the delivery and payment partners needed to complete your order, and we keep it only as long as necessary. If you have questions or want your information updated or removed, please contact us.
